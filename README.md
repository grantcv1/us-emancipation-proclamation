# The Emancipation Proclamation in Akoma Ntoso

The Emancipation Proclamation of January 1, 1863 (Proclamation 95), in Akoma Ntoso XML, with a CSS stylesheet that renders it after the engrossed original. It was generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `emancipation-proclamation.xml` | The Akoma Ntoso document |
| `emancipation-proclamation.css` | The stylesheet, linked from the XML |
| `index.html` | Landing page for GitHub Pages |

To view the document locally, keep the XML and CSS files in the same folder and open the XML in a browser.

## About the markup

- **Standard:** Akoma Ntoso 2.0 (AKN 3.1). The namespace is `http://docs.oasis-open.org/legaldocml/ns/akn/3.0`, with `version="3.1"` on the root element.
- **Validation:** The XML validates against the Akoma Ntoso 2.0 cs01 schema.
- **Document type:** The Proclamation is an `<act name="proclamation">`, with `FRBRsubtype` set to `proclamation` and `FRBRnumber` set to 95.
- **Structure:** The heading is in `<preface>`. The "Whereas" clause and the two passages it quotes from the preliminary proclamation of September 22, 1862 are in `<recitals>`, with the quotations in `<embeddedText>`. The six paragraphs of the order are in `<body>`, with `<paragraph>` elements that are editorial, since the original has no numbering. The witness clause, date, seal and signatures are in `<conclusions>`.
- **Places and dates:** The ten states named are marked with `<location>`. The excepted parishes and counties are not marked. Dates are marked with `<date>`.
- **Supplied text:** The closing parenthesis that the National Archives supplies in brackets is in `<span class="supplied">`.
- **Highlight:** The words "are, and henceforward shall be free" are in `<span class="landmark">`. The emphasis is editorial and is not in the original.
- **Closing lines:** The transcription prints "By the President: ABRAHAM LINCOLN" and then "WILLIAM H. SEWARD, Secretary of State." On the original, Lincoln signs first, and "By the President:" introduces Seward. The XML follows the original in this one respect; the words are the transcription's.
- **Identifiers:** Every element with an `eId` also has a `GUID`. Text-level `<p>` elements have neither.
- **Images:** The seal and the two signatures are images embedded in `<img>` elements. Browsers do not draw Akoma Ntoso `<img>` elements, so the CSS repeats them as backgrounds. The signature images have had the paper removed, and the seal has a soft edge.

## Sources

- Text: the U.S. National Archives [transcription](https://www.archives.gov/exhibits/featured-documents/emancipation-proclamation/transcript.html). Spelling and punctuation follow that transcription, including "Berkley" and "self-defence".
- Paper, seal and signatures: the National Archives [scan of the original](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/299998), National Archives Identifier 299998, page 5.

## Fonts

The stylesheet embeds Pinyon Script and Petit Formal Script, both licensed under the [SIL Open Font License](https://openfontlicense.org/). The fonts approximate the clerk's hand; they do not reproduce it, and line breaks differ from the original.
